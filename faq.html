Frequently Asked Questions

What dumpster sizes do you offer?

We offer 2, 4, 6, and 8 yard dumpsters.

Do you accept construction trash?

No. We only accept residential, regular, and commercial trash. No construction debris.

Do you offer residential and commercial service?

Yes. We offer both residential and commercial trash service.

What areas do you service?

Residential service is mainly outside the city limits of Angleton and Lake Jackson, including nearby areas like Danbury and Holiday Lakes. Commercial service also includes Rosharon and Highway 6 areas.

How do I request a dumpster?

Call us or come by our office to check dumpster availability.

How long can I keep a dumpster?

Long-term contracts are usually 1 to 2 years. Temporary dumpsters can be rented for one week or longer depending on your needs.

How often is trash picked up?

Pickup depends on the service type and area. Call our office for details.

What items are not allowed?

No construction debris, batteries, motor oil, oil filters, whole tires, gasoline, hazardous chemicals, or other restricted materials. Call us if you are unsure about an item.

How much does dumpster service cost?

Call our office for a quote.

Do I have to sign a contract?

Yes. Temporary and long-term dumpster rentals both require a signed contract.

Do you deliver dumpsters?

Yes. Dumpster delivery has a delivery fee. Customers can pick up a 96-gallon cart at our office to avoid the delivery fee.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Holiday hours may change and we will send out a notice if we are closed.
